Please note

Safety & General Instructions

A short list worth reading before you ride out. It keeps your trip smooth and everyone safe.

Timings

  • Please return to your hotel by 10:00 pm.
  • On the ECR road, do not drive later than 9:00 pm on the road to Auroville.
  • The rental office closes at 9:00 pm on all days. Plan your return accordingly.

On the road

  • Do not drink and drive.
  • Always wear a helmet.
  • Carry your driving licence at all times.
  • Be alert at all times and avoid rash driving.
  • Double-check before taking a right, left or U-turn.

Looking after yourself

  • Do not leave valuables in the scooter storage.
  • Do not drink and swim at the beaches. Stay safe and be alert.

Where you can ride

  • Scooters are for local sightseeing only.
  • Maximum range: 20 km.
  • Driving to Pichavaram and Mahabalipuram is not allowed.

Before you ride

You Will Have To

  1. Deposit your original valid photo ID

    Accepted examples:

    • Aadhaar Card
    • Voter Card
    • Driving Licence
    • Passport (foreign visitors)

  2. No security deposit

    We do not collect a separate security deposit.

  3. Pay the rental in advance

    The total rental amount is payable in advance.

Rental terms

Key Rental Rules

  • Obey all traffic rules. No Entry, One Way and No Parking rules must be followed.

  • Traffic violations are the renter’s responsibility.

  • Park securely at night. Lock the vehicle and park it in a secure place.

  • Lost key or lock set: ₹900.

  • Lost helmet: ₹500.

  • Loss, damage or theft of the vehicle or its parts is the renter’s responsibility.

  • Do not drive triples.

  • Punctures: get the puncture sealed. Please do not change the tube or tyre expecting reimbursement.
