Safety Standards

Our Commitment to Rider Safety

Your safety is non-negotiable. Please review our essential guidelines for a secure, hassle-free ride.

Office & Riding Hours

  • Rental office closes at 9:00 PM on all days.
  • Riders are requested to return to their hotel by 10:00 PM.
  • Avoid riding late night on ECR towards Auroville after 9:00 PM.

Road Rules & Law

  • Always wear your helmet while riding (driver & pillion).
  • Strict zero tolerance for drink & drive.
  • Carry your original driving licence at all times.
  • Observe one-way signs, no-parking zones & speed limits.

Local Riding Range

  • Scooters are strictly for local sightseeing (max range: 20 km).
  • Riding to Pichavaram or Mahabalipuram is not permitted.
  • Keep your vehicle locked securely overnight at hotel parking.

Before you ride

What You Will Need

Deposit Original Valid Photo ID

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Voter Card
  • Driving Licence
  • Passport (Foreigners)

Valid Driving Licence

Carry your original driving licence with you at all times while operating the two-wheeler.

Rental terms

Key Rental Rules

  • Obey all traffic rules. Follow No Entry, One Way in White Town, and No Parking signs.

  • Traffic violations & fines are strictly the renter’s responsibility.

  • Park securely at night. Always lock the vehicle and park inside hotel premises.

  • Lost key or lock set replacement: Flat ₹900 fee.

  • Lost helmet fee: Flat ₹500 fee.

  • Do not ride triples. Strict compliance with passenger limits.

  • Punctures: Please get puncture sealed locally. Tube/tire replacements are not reimbursable.
