Our Heritage

Five Decades on the Same Streets

Established in 1973 on Thennanjalai Road in Puducherry, Vijay Arya Bike Rental began with a simple mission: to give visitors affordable, flexible, and safe mobility to explore the town.

Over the decades, as Pondicherry evolved into a beloved coastal destination, generations of travellers, university students, and families have started their journey from our counter. We remain a proud family business rooted in local hospitality and honest service.

Safety Standards

Our Commitment to Rider Safety

Your safety is non-negotiable. Please read our key guidelines to ensure a smooth and enjoyable trip.

Office & Riding Hours

  • Rental office closes at 9:00 PM on all days.
  • Riders are requested to return to their hotel by 10:00 PM.
  • Avoid riding late night on ECR towards Auroville after 9:00 PM.

Road Rules

  • Always wear your helmet while riding.
  • Never drink and drive under any circumstances.
  • Carry your original driving licence at all times.
  • Observe one-way signs, no-parking zones, and speed limits.

Local Riding Range

  • Scooters are strictly for local sightseeing (max range: 20 km).
  • Riding to Pichavaram or Mahabalipuram is not permitted.
  • Keep your vehicle locked securely overnight.
